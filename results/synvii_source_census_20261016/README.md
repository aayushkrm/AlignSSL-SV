# Published synVII source inventory

This is a source-availability check, not an experiment or phenotype analysis.
[Table S3](source-table-s3.xlsx) is an unchanged18,157B copy of the published
strain inventory. [Receipt](receipt.json) binds source version, bytes, hashes
and the observed lexical counts. It does not provide the full219-clone panel.

Attribution: Shen et al., *Dissecting aneuploidy phenotypes by constructing
Sc2.0 chromosome VII and SCRaMbLEing synthetic disomic yeast*, Cell Genomics,
2023. [Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xgen.2023.100364),
[official PMC source](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC10667312.1/mmc2.xlsx).
Current PMC metadata reports CC BY. The two downloaded PDFs remain outside Git;
their public paths and checksums are recorded in the
[actual census note](../../docs/research/2026-10-16-synvii-source-census.md).

Counts use original Sheet1 column A identifiers and column D comments. The
extra formatted blank row137 is not a strain. Counts are not independent
clones/replicates by assumption. No workbook, formula or figure was edited.

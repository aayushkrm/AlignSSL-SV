# HGSVC3/PAV callable-mask availability

**Checked:** 2026-09-23
**Question:** Do the HGSVC3 `20240307_PAV_VCF` working archives or the v1.0 release provide donor- and haplotype-specific, reference-coordinate callable BEDs suitable for confident-negative SV labels?

## Finding

A reported metadata-only HTTP Range walk of the two official `20240307_PAV_VCF` TAR archives reached their TAR end markers and found zero `.bed`, `.bed.gz`, or `.bed.bgz` members and zero member paths containing `callable`. This observation applies only to those two named archives; independent replication remains pending because the scanner and raw member log have not yet been archived.

The HGSVC3 v1.0 release is a merged variant callset, not a genome-wide negative mask. Its `.` genotype allele means that the corresponding assembly haplotype was not callable at that represented variant locus; it does not mark the callable genome. PAV 2.4.0.1 can generate a per-assembly, per-haplotype reference-coordinate callable BED, but the reported survey did not find those outputs in the two working archives. PAV 3 documents separate reference- and query-coordinate Parquet outputs; those are a different software version and are not evidence about the contents of the 2024 PAV 2 archives.

## Working archives: member-level result

The official [working-directory listing](https://ftp.1000genomes.ebi.ac.uk/vol1/ftp/data_collections/HGSVC3/working/20240307_PAV_VCF/) lists `20240307_PAV_VCF_hg38_mm2.tar`, `20240307_PAV_VCF_hs1_mm2.tar`, a README, and a manifest. The [README](https://ftp.1000genomes.ebi.ac.uk/vol1/ftp/data_collections/HGSVC3/working/20240307_PAV_VCF/README_20240307_PAV_VCF) calls these “PAV intermediate variant calls”: PAV 2.4.0.1 development calls from 65 phased assemblies, with `h1`, `h2`, and unassigned (`un`) haplotypes. It says failed-QC variants are omitted and the PASS callsets were being used in HGSVC3 merges and QC. The README does not claim to package complete PAV run directories.

The official [manifest](https://ftp.1000genomes.ebi.ac.uk/vol1/ftp/data_collections/HGSVC3/working/20240307_PAV_VCF/MANIFEST_20231211_Freeze1_20240307) provides whole-file lengths and MD5s, but no TAR member index:

- `20240307_PAV_VCF_hg38_mm2.tar`: 12,630,108,160 bytes; MD5 `c4d379205f4fb3162de89a09abb12dca`.
- `20240307_PAV_VCF_hs1_mm2.tar`: 12,857,282,560 bytes; MD5 `167bb858385c36e64d27ab68ef4472cb`.

The earlier survey reported that HEAD responses for both archive URLs advertised `Accept-Ranges: bytes`; their `Content-Length` values matched the manifest, and their ETags remained stable during the walk. Its member-header walk reportedly requested 512-byte TAR headers, skipped each payload by its declared size, required HTTP 206 and consistent `Content-Range`, and reached both zero-block TAR end markers. Per archive it reported 391 member headers (390 regular files and one directory): 195 `.vcf.gz` members, 195 `.tbi` members, zero BED members, and zero names containing `callable`. It reported 393 range requests and 201,216 TAR bytes per archive, with no member payload fetched. Without the scanner and raw member log, this is a provisional absence claim limited to the two named archives.

## v1.0 release: merged calls are not a negative mask

The official [v1.0 README](https://ftp.1000genomes.ebi.ac.uk/vol1/ftp/data_collections/HGSVC3/release/Variant_Calls/1.0/README_Variant_Calls_1.0.md) describes calls from 65 phased genomes made with PAV 2.4.0.1, merged with SV-Pop, and QC-supported by other callers; it says unreliable regions including centromeres and telomeres were discarded. The release provides GRCh38-NoALT and T2T-CHM13 references. Its reference-level VCFs carry h1 and h2 genotypes. The README explicitly defines `.` as a haplotype that was not callable because its assembly did not align at that variant locus.

The official [v1.0 root listing](https://ftp.1000genomes.ebi.ac.uk/vol1/ftp/data_collections/HGSVC3/release/Variant_Calls/1.0/) lists the two reference directories, README, manifest, and annotation workbook. The [GRCh38 root listing](https://ftp.1000genomes.ebi.ac.uk/vol1/ftp/data_collections/HGSVC3/release/Variant_Calls/1.0/GRCh38/) lists VCFs and their `.csi`/`.tbi` indexes, plus `annotation_table/` and `vcf_with_unphased/`; it lists no callable BED at that level. The separate [Assembly_Info v1.0 listing](https://ftp.1000genomes.ebi.ac.uk/vol1/ftp/data_collections/HGSVC3/release/Assembly_Info/1.0/) lists a README, manifest, and Verkko assembly/QV tables, not callable intervals. These listings do not justify assigning reference genotypes to absent VCF rows. The HGSVC [phase3 README](https://github.com/hgsvc/phase3-main-pub/blob/main/README.md) distinguishes its broad `working/` area, which can hold unpublished intermediates, from `release/`, which holds essential production-quality resources such as callsets.

## What PAV 2.4.0.1 can generate

The pinned [PAV 2.4.0.1 README](https://github.com/EichlerLab/pav/blob/v2.4.0.1/README.md#L150-L164) describes `results/{asm_name}/callable/` BEDs as regions where contigs aligned, smoothed by 500 bp. The [tagged call workflow](https://github.com/EichlerLab/pav/blob/v2.4.0.1/rules/call.snakefile#L183-L215) names the output `results/{asm_name}/callable/callable_regions_{hap}_{flank}.bed.gz`. Its callable-region rule combines a per-haplotype `trim-tigref` alignment BED with large deletion, insertion, and inversion intervals; the final haplotype-merge rule requests the `_500` BED.

The [alignment workflow](https://github.com/EichlerLab/pav/blob/v2.4.0.1/rules/align.snakefile#L53-L75) labels `trim-tigref` as reference-coordinate trimming and calls it with `mode='ref'`. PAV's [`get_align_bed` implementation](https://github.com/EichlerLab/pav/blob/v2.4.0.1/pavlib/align/align.py#L666-L730) sets `#CHROM`, `POS`, and `END` from the SAM reference name/start/end, supporting the conclusion that this output is in reference coordinates. The [region-merge implementation](https://github.com/EichlerLab/pav/blob/v2.4.0.1/pavlib/util.py#L36-L100) pads intervals for the merge decision, then removes the pad at output endpoints. With the `_500` output, this can bridge an unaligned gap of up to 1,000 bp between input intervals. Thus the merged BED can include bases absent from the original per-haplotype reference alignment.

These generated BEDs are a plausible alignment-derived callability input, not a publisher-certified high-confidence-negative benchmark. The smoothed BED alone cannot certify base-by-base alignability of a proposed negative. PAV's separate `FILTER_h1`/`FILTER_h2` input BEDs mark unreliable regions on the assembly and are explicitly in **assembly coordinates**, as the [PAV input documentation](https://github.com/EichlerLab/pav/blob/v2.4.0.1/README.md) states. They must not be confused with the reference-coordinate callable output. The reported archive survey did not find this PAV 2 output in either `20240307` TAR.

## PAV 3 is a separate route

The inspected [PAV 3 README at commit `fdc89c97434fb1de2378ed61253286b69c1734c5`](https://github.com/BeckLaboratory/pav3/blob/fdc89c97434fb1de2378ed61253286b69c1734c5/README.md#L66-L73) describes `results/NAME/call_hap` as unmerged per-haplotype results and says it includes callable-region Parquet tables in reference (`callable_ref`) and query (`callable_qry`) coordinates. The [workflow at that same commit](https://github.com/BeckLaboratory/pav3/blob/fdc89c97434fb1de2378ed61253286b69c1734c5/src/pav3/data/workflow/rules/call.smk#L201-L255) creates those tables from reference- or query-coordinate alignment-depth tables and filters to depth greater than zero. Those table names, format, and implementation differ from the PAV 2 BED output; PAV 3 documentation does not establish that the PAV 2.4.0.1 working archives contain PAV 3 products.

## Requirements for a confident negative

A missing variant row, or a PAV `0|0` genotype at some other reported site, is not enough to label a read-based DEL candidate false. A defensible negative needs a frozen candidate representation and a donor-specific, haplotype-specific mask on the exact same reference build and contig naming scheme as the candidate coordinates. The full normalized deletion interval and prespecified breakpoint flanks must be callable on **both** assembly haplotypes. For each haplotype, validate coverage against the underlying unsmoothed, trimmed reference-alignment intervals rather than the `_500` BED alone; exclude bridged unaligned gaps, assembly gaps, alignment breaks or ambiguity, low-quality regions, and assembly-QC-filtered loci. Map any assembly-coordinate QC exclusions into reference coordinates before applying them. Compare the candidate to assembly calls with representation-aware matching so a shifted, split, or complex event is not mistaken for absence.

Treat every missing haplotype, uncertain alignment, masked interval, reference mismatch, or unresolved event representation as **unknown**. PAV assembly callability also does not establish short-read evidence quality: a claim that a short-read caller missed a real variant needs adequate read-side coverage and mapping support at that interval. Before training on negatives, validate the frozen rule against independent, blinded truth, include known DEL controls, and report the observed error count and its uncertainty.

## Search scope, limitations, and next step

This check covered the official `20240307_PAV_VCF` listing, README, manifest, and a reported full TAR member-header survey; the v1.0 `Variant_Calls/1.0` and `GRCh38` root listings and release README; the `Assembly_Info/1.0` root listing; the HGSVC PAV 2.4.0.1 documentation/source; and the pinned PAV 3 README. It did not recursively enumerate every child of the v1.0 annotation directories, inspect other HGSVC3 `working/` archives or restricted areas, or search external donor repositories. No CRAM or TAR member payload was downloaded. The two-TAR absence result is provisional until the member walk is independently reproducible; it says nothing about other HGSVC3 working or external data.

No full-TAR transfer is warranted for this question. The range-walk procedure and counts are described above, but a runnable scanner and raw member log have not yet been archived; independent replication of that survey remains an open reproducibility task. If an actual mask is needed, the lowest-cost next step is to verify the provenance and checksum of a PAV 2.4.0.1 callable BED for one selected donor and both haplotypes, if it exists outside these archives; otherwise rerun the pinned PAV version on the selected phased assemblies and exact reference. Then audit its raw alignment coverage, bridged gaps, QC exclusions, full candidate intervals and breakpoint flanks, and independent truth performance before assigning any negative labels.

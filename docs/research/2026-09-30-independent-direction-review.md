## High-impact decision

Prioritize **genotype-confidence transfer over a fixed known-SV catalog** as a
question to falsify. The only selected current stage is checking usable input
availability without comparing predictions to truth. The existing SSL thesis
remains suspended. Generic ensemble/matcher work is supporting infrastructure,
not the thesis. Nested-repeat discovery remains a higher-upside alternative
only after orthogonal evidence of genuinely missing alleles beyond equivalent
representations and existing local-assembly/repeat tools.

[SVUPP](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12771361/) already tests
GQ-ranked errors, matched numbers of accepted calls, depths/platforms, and poor
quality-ranked performance for neighboring SVs. Reproducing those observations
would not constitute our contribution. The unresolved possibility is reliable
prospective decisions on **unrelated** donors beyond ordinary calibration and
existing genotyper safeguards. An earlier suggested diploid-neighborhood veto
was demoted to an exploratory control because allele-neighborhood/partial-path
safeguards already exist in modern genotypers. No distinct residual mechanism
has been established.

The one Guo development archive and small SVUPP pipeline inspection were
bounded availability checks. The Guo archive's published MD5 and ZIP integrity
passed; its 14 pre-final VCF headers were inspected, not scored. Its 103 members

## Independent rigor clarification

The Sol/high-requested reviewer accepted the **conditional genotyping
estimand**, not a scientific result: a predefined, independently genotyped
catalog can include supported reference genotypes without labeling genome-wide
absence. Freeze eligible donor–catalog pairs using truth confidence and
callability independently of evaluated predictions. Track missing records,
missing GTs and their causes; neither becomes `0/0`.

Report together:

- GT decision coverage: accepted decisions / eligible donor–locus pairs.
- Selective GT risk: incorrect accepted GTs / accepted decisions.
- Correct-GT yield: correct accepted GTs / eligible donor–locus pairs.

These are not candidate recall or end-to-end discovery precision. Event QUAL
is not genotype confidence; GQ, GP and likelihoods require caller-specific
semantics before an expected-error interpretation. Representation reconciliation
must not count partial allele agreement as correct genotype dosage.

The reviewer also distinguished the exhaustive script's retrospective
truth-conditioned representation from the iterative script, which emits actual
SVDB-merged VCFs but selects caller additions with truth metrics. Legitimate
exploratory best-case searches do not establish deployment performance; freeze
selection and evaluate separately. Do not extend one script's limitations to
all article methods or conclusions.

## Gates before any scoring

The proposed first scoring falsifier is **no-fit donor transfer**, only if
verified, task-aligned inputs exist. Choose modality before outcomes. Freeze
one modern genotyper's confidence rule, eligibility, allele-equivalence rules,
threshold and deterministic tie handling on development evidence; apply
unchanged to an unrelated development donor with trusted catalog GTs. Keep
confirmation donors untouched. Error minus caller-interpreted expected error
must be accompanied by coverage and correct-GT yield over the full denominator.

Stop or redirect if interpretable confidence, trusted GTs or unrelated
out-of-training predictions are unavailable; if equivalence or existing
safeguards explain the error; or if confidence already transfers adequately.
Only a surviving failure motivates a separately frozen source-only calibration
comparison. Count its labels in the budget. If ordinary calibration suffices,
reject the learned-uncertainty thesis. Insufficient independent loci means
inconclusive, not a manufactured null or positive result. Freeze uncertainty,
multiplicity and scoring/compute limits before that experiment, with a further
independent review. No GPU campaign is authorized by this review.

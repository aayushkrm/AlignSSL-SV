# New-evidence SV opportunity scout — 2026-10-16

## Decision

**Scoped disposition: no new publication opportunity is selected from this screen.** This is not a claim that the SV field is exhausted. The rare-specific-allele versus near-identical-background mixture question was already triaged: MIMS distinguishes an easy collapsed benchmark from a hard allele-resolved benchmark, requires joint germline/somatic matching, and has explicit stop gates. Do not rename that question as a new lead. New SMaHT metadata may make a small measurement feasible; that would change feasibility, not the scientific claim. This note does not approve an additional experiment.

The evidence window was **2025-10-16 through 2026-10-16**. “Public result” below means a paper or landing page can be read; it does not mean every underlying read or label is openly downloadable.

## Why no residual diagnostic is selected

The three COLO829 junctions remain a possible table-level coverage question, not an established SMaHT error or a selected project. The available evidence does not yet show whether these exact structures are in the released benchmark truth, represented as complete events, or scored in the relevant outputs. Event absence, split representation, or an unscored row would need to be separated from donor/sample mismatch, coordinate conversion, benchmark scope, and unresolved truth. None is evidence of a false call by itself.

The public [COLO829 author repository](https://github.com/StergachisLab/T2T-COLO829BLT-Manuscript) contains small source and quantitative artifacts; the named 40.4-KB script is a read-level unit-size indel scan, not a benchmark comparator. A repository inventory reported by main also identifies a small centromere-analysis artifact. These facts reduce data-preparation cost. They do not establish a new independent label, a caller failure, or a consequential residual. The primary SMaHT portal policy lists COLO829 FASTQ/BAM plus germline and somatic variants as open; a broader protected-donor-tissue statement must not override that COLO829-specific listing. Exact item IDs, file availability, and registration flow still need verification. This does not make COLO829 a controlled physical mixture. The [HapMap mixture archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19636324) is separate and is not COLO829. Access metadata may make a bounded crosswalk runnable; it changes feasibility only. No crosswalk, dataset download, or experimental route is authorized by this scout.

The current-work limits on downloads or jobs are not scientific eligibility criteria for a later project. No candidate was rejected because a future cluster analysis might use more than 1 GB or need a job. Raw size is not a rejection reason. The present decision is narrower: this review did not find a new claim whose support exceeds prior art and whose outcome would change a scientific action. The donor-specific assembly study was also screened; it did not add a separately labeled residual to this screen. [Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12636593/).

## Claim limits

- High per-read accuracy and high depth at selected PureTarget targets do not establish genome-wide or guide-independent ascertainment.
- F1 inheritance validates germline transmission for the observed allele; it does not establish sensitivity to alleles that prevent capture.
- The reported 1,053-bp F1 deletion is an independent, cross-method positive. It is too short to reach the nearest listed capture guide from its cut site. Its recovery cannot be used as evidence that guide-site dropout is absent in general.
- An allele missing from a callset is not a false negative without independent evidence that the allele exists in that sample.
- Tissue prevalence, orthogonal support for a subset of breakpoints, and benchmark utility are separate claims. Do not treat one as proof of the others.
- MIMS's hard allele-resolved benchmark and joint germline/somatic matching are existing prior art for the rare-specific-allele mixture question. A newly accessible file or small input set changes feasibility, not novelty.
- The COLO829-T2T centromeric junctions are not known here to be missing from, or mis-scored by, SMaHT. No event absence is counted as a false negative.
- KOLF line-level expression differences are associations, not causal SV perturbation results. This limits the phenotype claim only. HSPC Strand-seq estimates surviving-cell outcomes, not initial event induction.

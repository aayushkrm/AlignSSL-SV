## Strong existing complete-structure prior art

[SVision-pro, online March22,2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-024-02190-7)
already forms read-ordered M/V/I candidates, sketches structure, recognizes
basic subcomponents, and joins them in read order. It compares case/control
component types, breakpoints and allele fractions. Main read these Methods
subsections, not all supplements/code. Simple junction linkage, component
composition or comparative allele fraction is therefore not an unoccupied
contribution. Those observations do not establish its performance on this case,
or turn allele fraction into independently validated purity/copy-number truth.

[pantree, Cell Genomics September9,2026](https://www.cell.com/cell-genomics/fulltext/S2666-979X(26)00189-8)
represents inversion edges individually because pairing can be unresolved,
including with assembly gaps. Its discussion distinguishes tree-dependent
variant representations from haplotype differences. Main read that targeted
discussion; edge pairing or change of reference basis alone is not a new idea.
This is not evidence that pantree is a read-based de novo inversion caller.

## Conditions for a useful residual

An important candidate residual is whether current fixed models predict
intervention-specific signed RNA changes with useful accuracy under a defined
structural/context regime, beyond an unchanged-gene prediction and strong
existing molecular controls. This tests an actual regulatory prediction use.
It is eligible for scrutiny, not yet a selected benchmark or a new mechanism.

First define the contrast: exact engineered allele versus matched parental or
sham condition, fixed gene/readout, and explicit units, copy state and context.
Carrier RNA alone is not a counterfactual; linkage, ancestry and cell state can
confound carrier/noncarrier differences. Intervention clones can also carry
secondary changes. Missing assays and nonsignificant effects are not zero truth.
For one gene with a common parental baseline, subtracting that baseline alone
does not create a new rank test.37 clones are not37 independent partner loci.

Use SVEN and AlphaGenome where the allele and relevant track are supported;
keep unsupported classes visible. SVEN's complete-SV-in-TSS-window rule cannot
be bypassed to score translocations as native failures. Orca-plus-ABC is the
strong contact-based control for Xu. Match allowed information: parental
chromatin is additional supervision; post-edit measured contacts cannot become
inputs to a claimed pre-intervention predictor. Keep activation, contact change
and RNA magnitude as separate tasks. Freeze scoring and cluster uncertainty by
partner locus; public benchmark reuse and model training exposure need disclosure.

The scouts overreach when diagnosis change becomes necessary for all value.
An independently resolved structural genotype, molecular effect, or measurable
calling improvement can justify research. Patient diagnosis is necessary only
for a clinical-utility claim. Retain their objections to equivalent anchors,
unsupported interval probabilities, repeated leaderboards and shared truth.
Assembly reuse creates dependence for assembly callers; it does not automatically
invalidate orthogonal Strand-seq support for a narrower orientation endpoint.
Neither clinical screen establishes a general molecular/methodological no-go.

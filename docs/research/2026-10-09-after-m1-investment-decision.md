## The single discriminating scientific evidence question

**Is there a reproducible class of independently supported, exact somatic repeat-allele changes in unrelated donors that remains unresolved after current strong ordinary controls, while the available prediction observations can distinguish those changes from inherited-allele selection, normal mosaicism and tumor copy change?**

This asks whether there is an identifiable scientific problem worth solving. It does not ask whether another archive can yield 16 rows, whether an old caller misses them, or whether a new score can separate published labels. A genuine residual would motivate investigation; it would not itself prove novelty, a new method, or publication value.

The exact missing scientific input is **an independent paired-allele challenge with supported positives and stable retained-haplotype controls, actual fair prediction observations, and case-resolved evaluation evidence**. A list of URLs or assemblies is insufficient. The input must jointly establish:

1. The normal and tumor structural alleles, the corresponding inherited haplotype, material/passage identity and relevant tumor copy state. Normal mosaicism must have a stated detection limit. If de-novo mutation is claimed rather than merely a tumor-normal difference, appropriate lineage evidence is needed; terminal paired sequencing cannot exclude every rare ancestral allele.
2. Evaluation evidence separate from the observations supplied to prediction, with assembly/read/curation dependence recorded. Stable controls require positive evidence that the relevant retained allele is unchanged. An absent call is not that evidence.
3. Complete observations required by the predeclared ordinary policies: a current phased/VNTR-aware somatic caller, diploid donor-specific evidence, and an applicable sequence-aware repeat/paired control. A finite ordinary combined policy is allowed; a truth-selected per-locus union is not. Inapplicability must be evidenced, not asserted from a tool's label.

The reviewed sources do not establish such a usable independent package. The retained metadata inventory also does not establish it. The six staged caller files cannot be converted into this material merely because they exist. No claim is made that the material is unavailable everywhere.

### Estimand and smallest real falsifier

The target estimand is the **net gain in correct whole tumor-normal structural-allele transitions over the strongest qualifying, fixed ordinary policy**, with false new transitions counted on positively supported unchanged alleles and within positive windows. Whole-event identity must include the sequence/haplotype/copy distinctions needed for the biological claim. Breakpoint proximity or a higher GQ is not the endpoint.

There is no verified denominator, eligible control pool or executable policy for a new test. I therefore do not invent a numerical minimum useful effect or a powered sample size. The old four-of-16 threshold belongs to the closed, development-exposed proposal; it cannot be transferred to a new donor collection or used as an acquisition entitlement.

The smallest meaningful *evidence* falsifier is one frozen, independently supported residual class assessed in at least two unrelated donors with supported unchanged counterparts. This is an operational replication requirement, not a power calculation or a publication sample size. It must test the same proposed failure mechanism, not unrelated convenient misses.

- If a qualifying ordinary whole policy resolves the class, stop that method rationale.
- If the observations do not distinguish a true allele transition from mosaic selection/copy change, stop an inference-method claim on those inputs. More compute cannot restore missing biological information.
- If truth or stable controls remain unresolved, return INCONCLUSIVE and stop; do not relabel them or hunt another source until a favorable residual appears.
- Only a supported, identifiable residual warrants a fresh scientific decision. It does not authorize training or a campaign. A distinct solution and untouched donor/locus-family validation would still be required.

This is the evidence that could reverse the stop, not an instruction to assemble it now. It is not a renamed M1: the historical 16, candidate 19, HG008 passages and exposed alleles cannot supply independent replication. No new donor, source or acquisition is selected in this note.

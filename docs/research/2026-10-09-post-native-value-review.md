## Scientific challenge

SVUPP Methods2.1 already compare GQ-ranked genotype discordance at matched accepted-call counts. Results3.2 already identify high discordance near neighboring SVs despite GQ filtering. A new reliability plot, repeat-stratified error curve, global score rescaling or matched-coverage comparison alone would therefore be weak added information. The fact that a score is imperfect does not establish a useful correction or a significant contribution. The opportunity ranking is a conditional judgment, not evidence that this lead is stronger than every alternative.

Probability calibration is distinct from rank discrimination. A monotone recalibration can improve probability estimates while leaving every accepted-call ranking, and hence the entire risk-versus-coverage curve, unchanged. Conversely, good ordering need not give trustworthy numeric probabilities. Do not count an unchanged ranking as a calibration failure, or a prettier calibration curve as improved selective genotype accuracy. Do not assume GQ is the unconditional probability that an entire diploid SV genotype is wrong: first verify each caller's score definition and conditioning. GL conversion also requires declared priors and allele space.

A potentially consequential question is narrower: **does one frozen correction predict exact genotype error beyond the already known GQ/depth/neighbor-SV effects, and does that improvement survive a genuinely independent test?** Safe abstention would require a reliable held-out error bound at useful coverage. Clinical significance does not follow from the application label or a retrospective reduction in discordance.

New sequencing, platform, center, library or cell line of the same donor is not independent biological replication.

## Findings and gates

1. **Blocker — unequal-base BED is not a safe exclusion or confident-callability mask.** The comparator writes an interval only where the two uppercase reference bases differ ([lines 62–80](../../scripts/compare_reference_contig.py)). For example, the chr14 BED has intervals ending at 16,228,649 and starting at 16,228,749 (BED coordinates); direct `samtools faidx` queries of the intervening 100 bases return `N` in **both** references. Thus filtering only BED overlaps would retain shared-`N` sequence inside a masked block. More generally, truth records and caller evidence can be affected by anchors, alignment, assembly, and candidate-window flanks outside an exact mismatch interval. **Gate:** derive and verify per-reference ambiguous/`N` regions, define the interval expansion and flanking policy from the actual caller/representation, audit overlaps with truth loci and candidate windows/read evidence, and separately establish donor-specific callable territory before using any absence as a negative. A BED pass alone does not authorize HGSVC3-versus-IGSR scoring or bulk CRAM transfer.

2. **High — source and artifact provenance remain incomplete.** The left FASTA has a verified publisher MD5 and locally rechecked SHA-256; the right side of this job is a live IGSR FASTA URL, while the recorded digest is for its **dictionary**, not a frozen FASTA snapshot. The JSONs store the URL but no content digest of the remote FASTA read by job `1598070`. During this review, a local uncommitted mirror appeared at [`results/reference_reconciliation/2026-09-23`](../../results/reference_reconciliation/2026-09-23), including `VERIFICATION.json`, the 18 pairs, and logs; I have not verified that mirror's transfer hashes or durable remote publication. Cluster scratch expires **2026-10-22** unless extended ([RESTART_STATE.md](RESTART_STATE.md)). Also, the wrapper skips any nonempty JSON plus existing BED without checking their paired hash ([lines 14–18](../../cluster/audit_reference_differences.sh)); today's manual verification does not protect a later restart. **Gate:** preserve a content-addressed IGSR FASTA (or validated per-contig sequence digests), the paired map files, source manifests, log, exact code/environment, and a machine-checked map manifest in durable storage before expiry; verify pairs on reuse. Do not silently regard a URL or dictionary hash as a FASTA snapshot digest.

3. **Medium — protocol documentation is one milestone behind.** The changed [reference reconciliation](2026-09-23-reference-reconciliation.md) still says job `1598070` is pending and that BED verification is next, although both are complete. Update it with this verification and the shared-`N` limitation, while keeping reference reconciliation and callable-negative gates explicitly open. The map covers the 18 M5-discordant contigs among 194 shared contigs; it does not evaluate IGSR-only ALT/decoy content or establish cross-reference read-alignment equivalence.

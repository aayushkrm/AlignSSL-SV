   It does not automatically disqualify its SV calls or orthogonal evidence

   labels are needed for lineage claims, not every SV detection diagnostic;
   lack of per-allele ddPCR does not alone invalidate an assembly-supported

not itself a reason that a diagnostic cannot be useful.

# Regulatory SV intervention data: Xu et al. (2022)

**Decision:** no runnable exact-allele counterfactual audit from the public Xu package. Keep the current HG002 STOP/PIVOT before S2. The publication supplies strong MYC-expression outcomes and an ABC baseline, but I found no public per-clone edited-junction sequence or small clone-by-outcome table. The accession-level metadata join is below; it does not supply exact model inputs or numeric per-clone expression values.

**Model setting:** Luna/max requested; runtime setting not independently attested.

MYC RNA is a valid mechanistic endpoint; a clinical diagnostic outcome is not required. The visible GEO manifest does not expose a small reusable numeric outcome table. Do not substitute CRISPRi, SNP MPRA, or primary-fitness screens for a DNA-SV-to-RNA contrast. The paper’s enhancer deletion is a separate perturbation, not a sham for the translocations.

## Existing prediction result and reuse risk

Xu et al. already report more than 500-fold MYC expression variation across clones. For the engineered set, nearest-enhancer distance gives AUC 0.25/0.182, aggregate enhancer activity 0.864, strongest-enhancer score 0.932, and Activity-by-Contact (ABC) 0.977. The TAD-aware ABC model reports 88% accuracy in its validation setting. ABC uses post-edit Hi-C contacts from engineered clones and H3K27ac from wild-type SK-N-DZ; it is not a DNA-sequence-only SV predictor and its post-edit contact input is unavailable before an edit. This is the strongest published Xu control to beat. [Results and methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9810360/)

The outcome labels and GEO data have been public since 2022. Exposure in any later model’s training corpus is possible but unverified. Do not treat a random clone split as independent: several clones share a partner locus. Group by partner locus. The set has only 15 partner regions and one cell-line lineage; it cannot support a broad cross-cell claim.

## Prior-art boundary and decision

Swap-seq is prior art for generic engineered-edit expression prediction (DOI [10.64898/2026.10.08.756856v1](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.08.756856v1)); details here were supplied by the main coordinator, not the human user, and were not re-reviewed by this worker. Main's source check and the independent reviewer separately read the primary model-scoring sections. It benchmarks designed deletions with inserted barcodes and compares AlphaGenome, Borzoi, and Enformer on signed REF/ALT effects. Do not claim a generic engineered-SV expression benchmark as novel.

**Residual beyond published controls: none that is currently defined and runnable.** Large interchromosomal translocations could be a distinct input class from Swap-seq’s short edits, but Xu lacks public exact edited alleles, a verified small numeric outcome table, and a matched editing sham. Xu’s ABC already reports AUC 0.977 using engineered-clone Hi-C. An outcome-only reanalysis would be a replication of Xu, not a well-defined counterfactual sequence test. Do not download the 104.5 GB archive or start an inference/infrastructure campaign.

The named adjacent paper, *Translocation-induced enhancer rewiring reveals cryptic oncogenic circuits in multiple myeloma* (PMID 42842680; PMC13644794), does not report engineered exact SV alleles with matched sham/reference expression outcomes, so it does not close the gap. [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13644794/)

Main integration qualification: measured post-edit Hi-C ABC is an additional-information control, not a fair sequence-only model that a DNA-only predictor must beat on identical inputs. The review also names Orca-predicted-contact ABC as a relevant pre-intervention control. This note's non-selection rests on no defined distinct runnable contrast and an unverified exact-allele/outcome crosswalk in the inspected public material, not a universal sham prerequisite, inaccessible-field proof, or unfair oracle-performance threshold. No archive acquisition follows.

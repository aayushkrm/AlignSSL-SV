# Functional deletions: investment decision and one stronger alternative

Date: 2026-10-12. Requested Sol6.1/max; model and effort are not independently attested.
Scope: one decision sidecar. Main retains integration; maintained review is required before a costly campaign.

## Decision

Do not select a Weller-only functional-SV modelling programme from the current evidence.
The three proposed questions do not yet offer a distinct publication contribution.
Rank one **conditional inversion-calling falsifier** above another functional-screen analysis.
Main is checking its primary source now. Use that result once; do not repeat the source audit here.
This recommendation selects a possible cheap test, not a pipeline or an experiment campaign.

The HG002 annotation-exclusion study stays stopped before S2. Parental SVA remains retired.
Neither the accepted readset nor the SSL/DeepSV code determines this decision.
Clinical outcomes, a new architecture, sham edits, or a pre-proved failure are not universal entry requirements.

That 336/450 is a threshold frequency, not sensitivity to 450 independently confirmed harmful alleles.

Their presence does not establish a per-construct endogenous edit fraction or an independent causal outcome.

## Why the functional questions do not rank first

| Question | Useful claim it could test | Investment decision |
|---|---|---|
| Held-out-locus fitness prediction beyond coding, gene essentiality, and CADD-SV | Predict this assay's construct depletion at unseen loci | Reject as a study lead now. Assay prediction could be useful, but no distinct design-budget or coverage benefit beyond the authors' editing model and annotation tests is defined. A cross-locus score gain alone would not establish that benefit or natural-SV function. |
| Edit-efficiency-aware effect inference | Separate failed editing from a tolerated deletion | Reject with these fields alone. Barcode survival combines editing, selection, and cell population history. Predicted efficiency is not measured endogenous editing. A time-course mixture fit needs an editing schedule and externally tested assumptions; otherwise different edit/effect combinations can fit it. Re-demonstrating that limitation is not a contribution. |
| Threefold tiling gives <3% false negatives for essential noncoding sequence | Establish reliable discovery at a stated screen budget | Strongest functional residual, but not an eligible sensitivity estimate yet. Essential-gene status does not make every partial exon deletion harmful. Geometry, independent functional interval truth, and the applicable claim must be established. Joint non-depletion can be measured without those facts, but it answers a narrower coverage question. |

The source-reported extrapolation is 70% coding hit rate plus three independent trials:
`(1 - 0.70)^3 = 0.027`. This is a conditional calculation, not an observed noncoding false-negative rate.
Variation across loci can invalidate a pooled calculation even when trials are conditionally independent.
Shared guides, overlapping target sequence, and biological tolerance can also produce joint non-hits.
Do not present those known possibilities, or a correlation plot, as a new result.
Do not count three constructs anywhere in a gene as threefold coverage of one required element.

A finite control-coverage analysis could inform assay planning, without proving noncoding sensitivity.
An assay-design method does not need a new biological mechanism; it does need a distinct useful result.
It would rank higher only if main's completed audit or the pending exon worker supplies a distinct,
testable claim with genuine overlap units and appropriate functional controls already available.
No extra source hunt, editing-model fit, or sequence-model download follows merely from the inventory.
Swap-seq already occupies the generic deletion-to-RNA benchmark and generic distal-prediction failure.

## Stronger conditional question: complex inversion recovery

The [balanced-rearrangement note](2026-10-12-balanced-rearrangement-opportunity.md) cites the August 2026
[INV-Benchmark](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-026-04223-7), five reference samples, nine callers,
Strand-seq plus phased assemblies, and [public truth](https://zenodo.org/records/17624133).
It reports Tier 2 recall near 50–68% for Severus/Sniffles2 with VACmap and 30–50% for PAV.
Main is verifying these comparisons and their denominators. They are not independently reproduced here.

This is a specific reason to test a calling mechanism. Missing phenotype blocks a diagnosis claim,
not a molecular calling study. The [October 7 STOP](2026-10-07-inversion-centromere-decision.md)
concerns inversion recurrence modelling against ferromic, not inversion-calling accuracy.
Using truth-building contigs as assembly-caller input makes the comparison dependent;
it can still test whether a caller recovers information present in those exact contigs.
It does not provide independent validation of every junction, copy count, or phase assignment.

Question: **Does a supported complex inversion remain unrecovered because a current method loses
the allele's junction linkage or orientation during alignment/decomposition, after equivalent call
representations and existing strong solutions have been accounted for?**
The gap is not established. Low benchmark recall alone does not identify its cause.

## One cheap falsifier, after main's source result

1. Fix one published missed Tier 2 case before new execution. Prefer the paper's named example.
   Otherwise freeze the first eligible case under a declared ordering, with all eligibility rules recorded.
   Require retrievable existing calls/input evidence, a resolved assembly interval, and mapped Strand-seq support.
   This is a selected diagnostic case, not an unbiased recall estimate. Keep that selection visible.
2. State two truth scopes. Strand-seq tests the supported orientation; assembly sequence supplies the
   detailed structural hypothesis. Treat assembly-conditioned sequence recovery as such. Do not upgrade
   orientation support to independent proof of all breakpoints, copy state, or haplotype linkage.
3. Compare the published/native calls as allele structures first. Accept equivalent representations in
   repeats. Check interval accounting, filters, callable territory, phase labels, and assembly gaps.
   Reconstruct the local alternate path where possible; a missing INV tag is not a missing inversion.
4. Challenge the residual with the strongest applicable current methods, including SVPG and SVision-pro,
   and the benchmark's best relevant caller/alignment combination. First use valid existing outputs.
   Any native case run must use documented supported inputs. Do not make an unqualified crop or adapter.
   Within each input track, give methods the same reference and observed evidence. Truth is evaluation-only.
   Disclose extra graph-panel information; do not use the evaluated truth allele to build a discovery graph.
   An incompatible method is untested, not failed. If strong comparators cannot be checked cheaply, HOLD.
5. For a residual failure, trace the evidence through alignment, candidate formation, linkage, and filtering.
   Ask whether the permitted input distinguishes the expected structure from a competing structure.
   Show the distinguishing sequence/alignment evidence, rather than infer it from the truth label.
   No architecture design or broad caller rerun is needed to answer this first case.

Fix the cause categories before interpreting outputs: correct allele in calls but missed by matching;
adequate native alignment but lost junction linkage during decomposition; uniquely informative contig/read
sequence that the alignment fails to retain; filtering; or insufficient input evidence. This separates
an evaluator problem from an alignment or inference problem. A source assembly discrepancy is another
category, not proof of caller failure. Existing alignment/mode rescues close the corresponding unmet-method claim.

Proposed diagnostic cap: one case, existing public evidence first, at most one CPU-hour of native work
and no new reads, models, or large graph acquisition. Main must cost the actual supported entry point.
This cap is a recommendation, not a job release. No new run occurs in this sidecar.
If the case needs a genome-scale setup, retain the uncertainty and stop this cheap test.

```text
fixed case + scoped truth + same permitted evidence
                         |
                compare allele structures
                         |
          equivalent calls? ---- yes ---> close the calling-gap claim
                         |
          current method resolves it? ---> close the unmet-method claim
                         |
          input distinguishes allele? --- no ---> scope an information limit
                         |
                        yes ---> trace a residual algorithmic mechanism
```

| Outcome | Scientific consequence |
|---|---|
| Equivalent structures recover the allele | Reject this claimed genetic failure. A matching/normalization correction alone is not the proposed paper. |
| SVPG or another strong method recovers it | Reject the unmet calling-gap premise for this case. Do not replace that comparator with a weaker one. |
| Gaps, insufficient linkage, or ambiguous input explain it | Reject an algorithmic rescue claim from this case; a truth label cannot add missing evidence. |
| Strong methods miss distinguishable structure | Retain a specific mechanism hypothesis. One case does not establish prevalence, generalization, or method superiority. |
| Inputs, support, or native execution cannot be qualified cheaply | Test unresolved. Do not convert access or interface limits into biological/caller failures. |

## What would justify further investment

For inversions: a reproducible residual mechanism beyond current SVPG/SVision-pro capabilities,
then the same frozen mechanism tested across independent loci/samples with precision as well as recall.
A useful intervention must improve structure recovery at equal permitted information, not only emit more calls.
Independent orientation evidence and assembly-dependent structure evidence must stay separate in the claims.
Maintained reviewer scrutiny must precede a costly campaign and acceptance of a major positive result.

For functional deletions: reconsider a completed worker result only if it supplies a distinct molecular
question and a verified allele/outcome contrast. No diagnosis requirement is added. No pending result is assumed.
Neither branch is publication-ready: Weller supplies no selected new contribution; the inversion mechanism
is still a hypothesis, and the existing benchmark already reports caller performance.

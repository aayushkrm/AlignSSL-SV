## Findings and gates

1. **Blocker — Reference differences are highly concentrated and cannot yet be masked defensibly.** The publisher-verified HGSVC no-ALT FASTA matches all 194 shared HGSVC3 VCF contig M5s; the IGSR dictionary matches only 176, despite equal lengths ([reconciliation](2026-09-23-reference-reconciliation.md); cluster `all_contig_m5.json`). I independently summed all 18 cluster `*_base_differences.json` files: **13,923,221 differing bases / 2,371,021,378 compared bases (0.587% across those 18 contigs)**. Six contigs account for 13,923,135 differences: chrY 2,778,688; chr19 2,674,361; chr5 2,282,185; chr22 2,087,802; chr21 2,050,051; chr14 2,050,048. By contrast, chr1 has 2 and chr10 has 36. Of the total, 13,923,127 are HGSVC A/C/G/T versus IGSR `N`; the remaining 94 are `N` versus ambiguity codes. The first recorded chr14 difference is at 1-based position 16,000,001. These counts directly refute a genome-wide “sparse differences” inference from chr1/chr10. The JSONs store **counts, difference types, and only the first 20 positions per contig**, not a full mismatch BED or position map. The prevalence of `N` suggests reference masking, but neither safe masking of *all* differences nor unaffected read alignment/candidate calling follows from these summaries. **Gate:** produce a complete, hash-linked interval/position map; audit affected truth records, candidate windows and flanks, reads, and callable territory against it; then validate one predeclared donor/interval with the exact reference or a documented remapping/coordinate-compatible bridge before transfer or scoring. Preserve the IGSR reference snapshot/digest as well as its URL. The proper treatment of these blocks remains unknown.

2. **Blocker — Confident negatives are absent.** The inspected HGSVC3 v1.0 variant and assembly-info inventories supply no donor-wide confident/callable BED ([callability audit](2026-09-23-hgsvc3-callability-audit.md)). A `0|0` at a listed variant or absence of a VCF row cannot label an arbitrary Manta candidate negative; `.` explicitly marks uncallable haplotypes at represented sites. The 64,428 DEL records ≥50 bp and 9,003–12,165 carrier records per donor are genotype inventory, not a candidate-label denominator ([genotype audit](2026-09-23-hgsvc3-genotype-audit.md)). **Gate:** obtain or derive reference-coordinate, donor-specific callable regions with assembly alignment/QC provenance, validate positive and negative labeling including flanks and representation-aware DEL matching, and report excluded territory and its length/repeat/ancestry distribution. If this fails, switch source. The alternative-cohort report correctly keeps HGSVC2 and HPRC provisional; its proposed HGSVC2 pilot must remain a metadata/callability feasibility test until this same gate passes.

3. **High — Donor independence and untouched confirmation are not yet established.** Exact index matching gives 63/65 public CRAM entries, not 63 independent or integrity-checked donors ([data decision](2026-09-23-data-decision.md)). The roster includes known trios, including NA19238/39/40; NA19238 has appeared in historical project work, and HG002/NA24385 is already development-exposed. Pedigree files may miss relatedness. **Gate:** reconcile all proposed IDs against prior exposure, verify alignment integrity and reference per donor, group relatives, freeze donor partitions and confirmation IDs before outcome inspection, and exclude confirmation donors and overlapping/multiscale loci from supervised training, SSL pretraining, model selection, normalization, calibration, and augmentation fitting. Record split IDs and the pretraining corpus manifest. Fresh labels on an exposed donor do not restore untouched confirmation.

4. **High — Fair comparison is specified but not executable or frozen yet.** [RESEARCH_PLAN.md](RESEARCH_PLAN.md) requires matched samples/windows, label budgets (including validation and calibration labels), training steps, optimizer-search and compute budgets, seeds, candidate pool, and scoring. No new Manta candidate VCF or learning result exists: `1598016` was **PENDING (Resources)** in both `squeue` and `sacct` at review; do not launch a duplicate. The 3 Mb HG002 fixture has only 11 v5.0q DELs ≥50 bp ([data decision](2026-09-23-data-decision.md)), so its output can test plumbing only. **Gate:** before a larger label-blind candidate run, freeze the Manta version/config and unfiltered candidate manifest; retain caller scores/filter decisions where emitted and the production-filter baseline. Compare matched scratch versus SSL for each representation, with logistic/boosted-tree 12-feature controls, depth-only, a clearly labeled DeepSV-representation reimplementation, and any feature-plus-embedding control, using the same splits, labels, search attempts, compute accounting, and per-seed predictions. A better corrected representation shared by scratch and SSL is not evidence of an SSL gain.

5. **High — Inference and claims must wait for a frozen endpoint and independent outcomes.** The plan names fixed-sensitivity precision, end-to-end call-set precision/recall, prevalence-aware PR curves, calibration, and cost, but the primary label budget, minimum useful gain, stopping rule, genotype/matching policy, candidate generator, exclusions, and confirmation IDs remain open. **Gate:** choose these from development data; count every attempted hypothesis, seed and label fraction; size confirmation using development effect/variance; predeclare comparisons and multiplicity correction; use donor/locus-grouped uncertainty rather than treating seeds or overlapping windows as independent patients. Fit thresholds and calibration only with budgeted development labels. Verify emitted VCFs with a frozen Truvari protocol and report strata, no-calls, duplicates, and calibration. There are no new biological scores; neither improvement, ancestry robustness, calibrated uncertainty, nor publication readiness is established by this milestone. Historical results remain development evidence.

6. **Medium — Reproducibility is time-limited.** Cluster `SOURCE.txt` and `SHA256SUMS` files exist for staged GIAB v5.0q, HGSVC3 v1.0, and HGSVC no-ALT inputs; the pilot has a `manifest.json`. I inspected their presence and source/purpose fields, and checked the reference JSON arithmetic, but did not rehash large staged files or traverse CRAM bodies. The reference-comparison code/wrapper and alternative-cohort report are uncommitted, and the 18 JSONs currently live on scratch. [RESTART_STATE.md](RESTART_STATE.md) records scratch expiry **2026-10-22**. **Gate:** mirror the full reference map, 18 JSONs, source/hash manifests, job logs, exact code revision, environments, candidate VCFs, split IDs, predictions and failures to durable project storage with verified hashes before expiry; record the remote IGSR FASTA identity. Do not let scratch retention become the sole provenance for a cohort decision.

**Open unknowns:** full mismatch intervals and their overlap with callable truth/candidate flanks; whether PAV working archives provide validated donor-specific reference-coordinate callable output; integrity/reference identity of each proposed CRAM; donor kinship beyond known pedigrees; whether any alternative source can pass the same gates; and the result of pending job `1598016`. None should be filled in by assumptions or by the current counts alone.
